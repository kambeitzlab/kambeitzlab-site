We are excited to share our new preprint - a collaborative work with our colleagues Ulrich Ettinger, Jason Schiffman and Kai Vogeley. In this work we use large-language models (LLM) to explore the semantic properties of questionnaires for the assessment of psychopathology. Our results indicate that LLMs can to a great part represent the empirical structure of psychopathology. This might offer a range of interesting application including the generation of better questionnaires, validating existing questionnaires or even informing empirical and theoretical research. https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-3347850/v1

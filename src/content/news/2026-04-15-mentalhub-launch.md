Together with partners, clinical colleagues and members of the community mental health network in Cologne, we celebrated the release of **MentalHub**: a great evening with many thoughtful conversations about recovery, peer support and the role of lived experience in mental health care.

At the heart of MentalHub are videos featuring honest, personal stories from experience experts. Formats like these can make psychoeducation more accessible, relatable and human.

The peer-led psychoeducation module is one part of a broader recovery aftercare approach. Next, a pilot study will show what effects we can observe.

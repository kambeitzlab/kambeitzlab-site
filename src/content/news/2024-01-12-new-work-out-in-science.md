Happy to have contributed to a recent publication in Science - a true team-effort led by Adam Chekroud. In a nutshell: a central open question in precision psychiatry is the extend to which prediction models can generalize to different samples. In our recent work, we generate models to predict treatment success with antipsychotics in schiozphrenia. While the models work well within the study they are trained on, they do not generalize to other studies. We hope that our results can facilitate the search for robust and generalizable markers in psychiatry...

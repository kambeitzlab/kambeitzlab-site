At the **AI Days 2026** of the University of Cologne, Joseph spoke about large language models (LLMs) in mental health.

Psychiatry is deeply language-driven: we diagnose, treat and understand patients through conversation, which makes LLMs particularly interesting for the field. The talk covered:

- language as a digital biomarker,
- how AI and clinicians make decisions,
- generative simulations of symptom trajectories.

The real challenge is not whether LLMs can do these things, but how we validate them rigorously and use them responsibly in clinical contexts.

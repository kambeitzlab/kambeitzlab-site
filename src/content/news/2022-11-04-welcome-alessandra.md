We are happy to welcome Alessandra Raio to our lab!

Alessandra is a PhD student from the University of Bari (Italy) where she works with Linda Antonucci, Guilio Pergola and Alessandra Bertolino. She joins us for a period of 6 months and will work on an exciting project to find out if developmental changes over time in cognition and personality might play a role in gene-by-environment interactions, contributing to psychosis susceptibility.

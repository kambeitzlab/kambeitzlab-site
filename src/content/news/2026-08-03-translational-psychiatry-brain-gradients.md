In our new study in *Translational Psychiatry*, we investigated how the hierarchical organisation of brain function is altered in people at clinical high risk for psychosis, with recent-onset psychosis and with recent-onset depression.

Using resting-state fMRI and functional brain gradients, we identified both **transdiagnostic alterations** across disorders and more **psychosis-specific changes**. The findings point towards a disturbed integration of sensory information, attention and higher-order brain processes, already in the early stages of illness.

Great work by Hannah Hacker, Linnea Hoheisel and the interdisciplinary PRONIA team.

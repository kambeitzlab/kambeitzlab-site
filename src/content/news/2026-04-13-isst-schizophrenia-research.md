Our paper on the ISST (Integrated Social Cognition and Social Skills Therapy) trial is out in *Schizophrenia Research*. The work comes from the ESPRIT network (Enhancing Schizophrenia Prevention and Recovery through Innovative Treatments) and examines which patients show greater gains in social functioning after cognitive remediation therapy.

This is exactly the kind of question that matters: not only whether a treatment helps, but for whom it helps most. A step towards more targeted, recovery-oriented interventions in schizophrenia.

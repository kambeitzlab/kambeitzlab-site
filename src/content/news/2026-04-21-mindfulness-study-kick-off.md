Great day in Berlin at the kick-off of a new DFG-funded multicenter study on **mindfulness-based group therapy for early psychosis**, led by Andreas Bechdolf and the team at Vivantes.

We look forward to working with all participating sites and contributing from our early recognition centre [FETZ Köln](https://psychiatrie-psychotherapie.uk-koeln.de/klinik/frueherkennungs-und-therapiezentrum-fetz/). A strong consortium for an important question.

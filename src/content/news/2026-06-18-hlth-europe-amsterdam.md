At **HLTH Europe** in Amsterdam (15–18 June 2026), Joseph gave a talk on *Modelling Mental Health with Generative AI Agents*: what if we could simulate how social and environmental factors shape mental health before running the study, before the intervention, at population scale? LLM-based generative agents let us start to do exactly that.

HLTH brought together a great mix of clinicians, engineers and entrepreneurs. There was lots of momentum in ambient scribing, speech-to-text and LLM-based documentation, and remote assessment.

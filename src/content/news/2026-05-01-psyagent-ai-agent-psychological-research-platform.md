We are developing PsyAgent - a research platform to model mental health in virtual environments using LLM-based generative agents. The concept is based on a previous publication by Park et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442. See also our previous work "Modelling the impact of environmental and social determinants on mental health using generative agents" https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-024-01422-z and our new preprint "LLM-Based Generative Agents Simulate Mental-Health Trajectories Under Adverse Socio-Environmental Conditions" https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-9291631/v1

Still work in progress but feel free to have a look!

https://github.com/joonspk-research/generative_agents

At the 39th ECNP Congress in Munich, Joseph joins the panel **"AI in Mental Health – Progress and Perils"**, chaired by Tim Hahn, alongside Raquel Iniesta, Paris Alexandros Lalousis and Ioana Podina.

Paris Lalousis will open by introducing the ECNP AI in Mental Health Network and its vision for responsible AI implementation across Europe. The panel then turns to the opportunities, limitations and risks of current AI systems, from bias and generalisability to governance and clinical implementation: how do we get from impressive AI demos to tools that actually improve patient care?

- **When:** Tuesday, 13 October 2026, 08:40–09:25
- **Where:** Room 14b, ECNP Congress, Munich

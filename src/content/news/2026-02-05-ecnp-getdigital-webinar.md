In the **GetDigital webinar** of the European College of Neuropsychopharmacology (ECNP), *Large language models in mental health: language-based modelling and generative applications in clinical neuroscience*, Joseph discusses how LLMs can be used to

- model mental-health-relevant language,
- support clinical and research workflows, and
- open new avenues for precision psychiatry and computational mental health.

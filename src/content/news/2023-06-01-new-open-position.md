We are looking for a new lab member! In an exciting interdisciplinary project (2 years) including psychiatry and linguistics in Cologne, we plan to investigate language in patients with psychosis. The project involves a collaboration with Prof. Vogeley (Psychiatry) and Dr. Cokal and Prof. von Heusinger (Institut für deutsche Sprache und Literatur I – Sprachwissenschaft).

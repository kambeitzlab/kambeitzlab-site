As part of the University of Cologne's **Mental Health Days** (7–9 December 2026, organised by EUniWell and Gesunde Uni Köln), Lana Kambeitz-Ilankovic, psychological lead of our early recognition and therapy centre [FETZ Köln](https://psychiatrie-psychotherapie.uk-koeln.de/klinik/frueherkennungs-und-therapiezentrum-fetz/), offers the workshop **"Small Talk – mehr als nur ein kurzes Gespräch"**.

Small talk helps build contacts and relationships, but it can be a source of real uncertainty. Difficulties often stem from social anxiety and negative automatic thoughts, such as fearing to say something wrong or to come across as boring. The workshop helps participants recognise these fears and thought patterns and build confidence in social situations. Through practical exercises and role-plays, small talk becomes a skill that can be learned.

The workshop is open to everyone interested.

- **When:** Wednesday, 9 December 2026, 10:00–11:30
- **Where:** Well-Being Lounge (Mensa Zülpicher Straße), Cologne
- **Registration:** until 2 December 2026

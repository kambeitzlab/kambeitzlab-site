Digital twins in mental health are still far from reality, but no longer pure science fiction. Our new paper in *Pharmacopsychiatry*, "Blocking the reflection: Milestones and hurdles for digital twins in mental health", looks at what would be needed to build meaningful digital twins in psychiatry: from multimodal data and longitudinal validation to trust, regulation and clinical usefulness.

AI may eventually help us build a "virtual patient", but a chatbot plus optimism is not yet a medical device.

Thanks to F. Gerrik Verhees and Pavol Mikolas for leading this effort.

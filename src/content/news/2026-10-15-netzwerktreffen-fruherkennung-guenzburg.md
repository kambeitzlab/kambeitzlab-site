Joseph joined the **6th Network Meeting on Early Detection and Intervention** in Günzburg, held alongside the symposium for the 10th anniversary of the LUI at the Bezirkskrankenhaus Günzburg.

It was a pleasure to catch up with Eva Meisenzahl, who shared exciting results from our CARE study, as well as Andreas Bechdolf, Frauke Schultze-Lutter, Andrea Pfennig, Maria Fernanda Urquijo and many other colleagues from Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Better mental healthcare for young people starts with working together.

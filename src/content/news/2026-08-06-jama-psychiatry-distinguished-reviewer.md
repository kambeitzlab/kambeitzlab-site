Joseph has joined the inaugural cohort of Distinguished Reviewers for *JAMA Psychiatry*, alongside colleagues such as Martin Paulus, Andre Brunoni and John Torous.

Peer review is the one job in science with no byline and no pay. It is good to see a journal put reviewers on the masthead and make this usually invisible work visible.

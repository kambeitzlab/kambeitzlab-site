We are thrilled to get started with the fantastic project PRESCIENT! It is an international network led by Prof. Barnaby Nelson in Melbourne. The aim is to create a unique dataset including numerous data modalities such as neuroimaging, language & activitiy markers or blood markers to create robust predictors for individuals at risk for psychosis.

After completing my Medicine Studies 2019 in Valencia (Spain), I perceived an opportunity working in Germany, so I started working as a medical doctor at the Department of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy of the University Hospital of Cologne. Beside my clinical activity, I go along with clinical research, currently focusing on the field of metaanalysis; in this case, the impact of childhood trauma as a transdiagnostic risk factor for various major psychiatric disorders.

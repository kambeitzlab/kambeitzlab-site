I studied medicine at the University of Cologne and since 2012 I'm working at the Department of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy at the University Hospital of Cologne. Since 2017 I started working at the Early Recognition an Intervention Centre for Psychiatric Disorders (FETZ) which is specialized in the early detection of individuals with a clinical high-risk for psychosis. I joined Kambeitz Lab as a clinican-scientist in 2019 and have expertise in supervising clinical-and other trials. My main interest is social cognition in schizophrenia and at-risk states.

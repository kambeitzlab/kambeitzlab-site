I studied at SRH Heidelberg (Germany) and the Ludwig-Maximilians-University Munich (Germany). During my master thesis and the first part of my PhD from 2017 till 2019, I gained scientific knowledge about machine learning and neuroscience in psychosis at the Munich site of the PRONIA multicenter study. I joined Kambeitz Lab in May 2019 and proceeded with my PhD project about the role of cannabis on brain development. Thereby, I am developing expertise in diffusion-imaging and I am involved in generating a robust pipeline for harmonization and processing of the DTI data in the PRONIA study.

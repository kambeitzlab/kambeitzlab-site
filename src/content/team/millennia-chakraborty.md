I hold a Bachelor's degree in Psychology from CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Delhi-NCR, India, which I obtained in 2022. Currently, I am pursuing a Master's degree in Experimental and Clinical Neuroscience at the University of Cologne. As part of my academic journey, I have joined the Kambeitz Lab, where I am focusing on conducting research for my master's thesis under the supervision of Priv.-Doz. Dr. Lana Kambeitz-Ilankovic. The primary objective of my thesis is to explore language and speech analysis in the context of psychosis. Specifically, I aim to investigate the semantic distinctions between experimental and control groups utilizing machine learning techniques.
